                    Three rules the ghost cannot break

                    1. It is given only commits. Messages, diffs, authors and dates from git history.
                    2. Every memory cites a commit. A short hash and the exact words from that commit.
                    3. The ward checks both. A hash that does not exist or a quote that is not there is struck out.

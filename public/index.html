            Three rules the ghost cannot break

            1. It remembers only commits. Messages, diffs, authors and dates from git history, nothing else.
            2. Every claim cites a commit. A short hash and, where it can, the exact words.
            3. The ward checks both. A hash that does not exist or a quote that is not there is struck out.

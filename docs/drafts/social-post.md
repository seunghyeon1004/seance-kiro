#KiroweenHackathon26 My favorite thing about Kiro: the specs. I wrote one sentence of intent and Kiro turned it into EARS requirements, a design and a task list that I could review before any code existed. Then the real model broke the mocked tests, and the spec told me exactly which requirement was wrong. Séance lets you ask the ghost of whoever wrote a line of code, and a ward checks every claim against the commits.
